deepdeep Linux environment third-party notices
==============================================

This file covers the Linux environment components shipped in the APK
(libproot.so, libproot-loader.so, Alpine minirootfs). The authoritative and
complete third-party notice is THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md at the repository root;
it is packaged at assets/licenses/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md. Full license texts
are packaged under assets/licenses/.

No root, no kernel, no VM
-------------------------
This is a user-space PRoot environment. It does not provide host root, a
separate kernel, container-grade isolation, or a complete Linux distribution.
